# SettlingUp privacy policy — draft for owner review

Effective date: set when the policy is published.

SettlingUp for iPhone helps you record shared expenses and calculate balances. It does not require a SettlingUp account, process payments, show advertising, or include analytics SDKs.

## Your information

The app stores the names you enter, tab names, expense descriptions, amounts, split details, and recorded payments on your device. When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit stores and synchronizes this information through your Apple account. Apple operates that service under its own terms and privacy policy. This policy makes no claim about end-to-end encryption of CloudKit records.

When you invite someone to a tab, that person can access its contents according to the sharing permissions. Only record information you are comfortable sharing with those participants. A text summary or backup you export goes to the destination you choose. Backups contain tab data and should be stored privately.

The native app does not send tab data to the developer's own server or to Supabase or Vercel. The older SettlingUp website is a separate service with different storage behavior; this policy describes the native iPhone app.

## Control and deletion

You can edit/delete expenses, undo a recorded payment, export a backup, or delete a private tab. Deletions sync when iCloud becomes available. To stop sharing or leave someone else's tab, use that tab's iCloud sharing controls. When an owner stops sharing, the app preserves a private owner copy; delete that copy separately if you no longer want it. Exported copies and information another participant has already copied are outside the app's control.

Deleting the app removes its local storage but does not necessarily delete iCloud records or exported backups. Manage remaining iCloud data using Apple's account storage controls. Keep a backup of records you want to retain before removing the app.
