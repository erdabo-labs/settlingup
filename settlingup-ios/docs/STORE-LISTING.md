# Store listing draft

Name: SettlingUp (subject to availability)

Subtitle: Split expenses with friends

Category: Finance or Utilities; owner to select.

## Description

Dinner, a weekend away, or everyday shared bills—keep the tab together and know who owes what.

Create a tab, add people, and enter expenses. Split evenly, enter custom amounts, or assign a personal expense. Include tax and tip, see clear balances, and record payments when money has actually changed hands.

Keep tabs on your iPhone, even offline. Invite friends through iCloud to collaborate, or send a simple text summary. Export a complete backup when you want a copy.

No SettlingUp sign-up, ads, or payment processing. Shared tabs require iCloud and the app. Changes synchronize automatically when Apple iCloud is available and may take time to appear. Amounts are in US dollars.

## Beta review notes

This is a native expense ledger. It does not transfer funds, connect to bank accounts, or sell financial products. No separate account credentials are required. Local features work without an iCloud sign-in. Collaborative sharing uses Apple CloudKit invitations between iCloud accounts; testing collaboration requires two accounts/devices. Version 0.1.0 does not import tabs from the older website.

## Owner must provide before submission

- Stable HTTPS support and privacy policy URLs, with a working contact destination.
- Final privacy answers reviewed against the binary and Apple's current questionnaire. The bundled manifest declares no tracking or collected data and declares the app's UserDefaults usage; verify these declarations for distribution.
- Screenshots for the device classes requested by App Store Connect, showing actual native screens.
- Copyright/owner details, age rating, final category, regions and distribution choice.

Suggested screenshots: tab library; expense entry and split preview; balances and payment recording; Duo expanded layout if accepted by the screenshot slot. Do not show another person's real names or financial records.
